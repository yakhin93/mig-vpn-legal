your device and our Tallinn-based servers. We do not log your traffic
(see the Privacy Policy).

## VPN technology

The service uses AmneziaWG, an obfuscated variant of the WireGuard
protocol. You acknowledge:
- VPN obfuscation is not a guarantee that every network will let our
  protocol through; censorship arms races mean availability may degrade.
- Some applications (banking, government services, location-restricted
  media) intentionally detect or refuse VPN traffic. MigVPN ships a
  bypass list that routes a curated set of services around the tunnel,
  but we cannot guarantee any specific app will work end-to-end.
